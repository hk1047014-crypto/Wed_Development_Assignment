Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their ID card while on campus.

Note: ID verification may be done at any time.

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for appearing in exams.

Attendance shortage will lead to disqualification.

Examination

Cheating or unfair means will result in immediate cancellation of the exam.

Students must arrive 15 minutes before the exam start time.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted on time.
Late submissions will not be accepted.

Deadline for current assignments: 31st August 2026.

Discipline

Misbehavior with faculty or peers will lead to strict action.

Respect and cooperation are expected at all times.
