Daily News: Major Discovery

Scientists uncover new planet in our solar system

In a groundbreaking announcement, astronomers revealed the discovery of a new planet orbiting beyond Neptune. This marks one of the most significant findings in modern astronomy.

The planet, temporarily named "Planet X," is believed to be nearly ten times the size of Earth. Researchers emphasized that this discovery could change our understanding of the solar system.

The official announcement was made on August 23, 2026, at the International Space Research Conference held in New Delhi. Scientists are now working on detailed studies to confirm its composition and orbit.
