Science Notes

Chemistry

Water formula: H2O

Carbon dioxide: CO2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Glucose: C6H12O6

Ammonia: NH3

Definition: Chemistry is the study of matter and its interactions.

Physics

Force = mass × acceleration (F = ma)

Work = Force × Distance

Energy = m × c2

Acceleration due to gravity = 9.8 m/s2

Volume expansion ∝ ΔT3

Key Concept: Physics explains natural laws governing the universe.

Biology

DNA structure: A-T, G-C base pairs

ATP molecule: C10H16N5O13P3

Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Cell division: Mitosis & Meiosis

Hemoglobin: C2952H4664N812O832S8Fe4

Important: Biology studies living organisms and their processes.
