Introduction

Technology is changing the way we study, communicate, work and solve real-world problems. GLA TechFest 2026 aims to encourage students to explore this changing world through practical learning and creative ideas.

The festival will provide students with opportunities to participate in technology-based activities and learn from other students who share similar interests.

The main announcement is that TechFest 2026 will bring multiple technology activities together under one student-focused event.

TechFest Highlights

Hackathon: Students can work in teams and develop creative technology solutions for real-world problems.

AI & Machine Learning Session: An introductory session about artificial intelligence, machine learning and their applications.

Robotics Showcase: Students can explore robotics concepts and see technology-based projects.

Coding Challenge: A programming-focused challenge designed to test logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Project Exhibition: Students can present their innovative college projects and explain how their ideas solve practical problems.

Technology & Innovation

TechFest encourages teamwork, creativity and practical problem-solving. Students can work together and turn their ideas into useful technology projects.
