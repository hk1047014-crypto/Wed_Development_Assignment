About Us

Code & Coffee is a fictional student-friendly café created for this HTML assignment.

It is imagined as a comfortable place for coffee, snacks, conversations and study breaks near a college campus.

Signature Items

Signature Item: Our Cold Coffee is a popular drink in this fictional café.

We also serve sandwiches, pastries and quick snacks.

Fresh bakery options can be paired with hot or cold drinks for a short break between classes.
