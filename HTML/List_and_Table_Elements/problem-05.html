Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest is a three-day celebration organized by the college for students. It includes technical competitions, cultural events, sports activities and creative challenges.

Registration Process

Follow these steps to register for a fest event:

  1. Choose the competition in which you want to participate.
  2. Open the online registration form.
  3. Enter your student details.
  4. Select the event category.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.

Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Participants must report before the event starts.
  • Students should follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Maintain discipline and cleanliness at the venue.
  • Participants should respect other teams and participants.
  • Any form of unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Students should take care of college property.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which students from different groups or categories can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants participate together.
Individual Event
An event where a single participant competes.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing a particular event or activity.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final result.
