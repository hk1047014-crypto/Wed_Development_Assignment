Before a Practical Class

Students should follow these steps before entering the laboratory:

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  3. Check that your computer system is working.
  4. Listen to the faculty instructions carefully.
  5. Complete the practical task.
  6. Save your work and submit it before leaving.

Classroom Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory for scheduled classes.
  • Students should arrive on time.
  • Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode.
  • Students should maintain classroom cleanliness.
  • Respect teachers and classmates.
  • Laboratory equipment must be handled carefully.
