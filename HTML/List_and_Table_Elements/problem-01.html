University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This page provides important information about eligibility, admission process, required documents, fees and contact details.

Admission Overview

GLA University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Students can apply for admission according to the eligibility requirements of their chosen course.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the eligibility requirements of the selected program before applying.

  • 10+2 qualification from a recognized board.
  • Required subjects should be studied according to the selected course.
  • Students must meet the minimum percentage requirement.
  • Valid documents must be provided during admission.

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete the admission process:

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Register yourself using your basic details.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Check the admission status and follow further instructions.

Required Documents

The following documents may be required during the admission process:

  • Class 10 marksheet
  • Class 12 marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Government ID proof
  • Category certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions a student must satisfy to apply for a course.
Merit List
A list of students prepared according to their qualifying performance.
Counselling
The process in which students receive guidance and complete course or admission choices.
Scholarship

Registration
