Important Sports Terms

Match
A competitive game played between two individuals or teams.
Fixture
The scheduled date, time and opponents for a sports match.
League
A competition where teams play multiple scheduled matches.
Knockout
A competition format where a participant is eliminated after losing according to the tournament rules.
