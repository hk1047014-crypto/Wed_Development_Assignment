Recruitment Process

  1. Submit your application.

  4. Technical interview.
  5. HR interview.
  6. Final offer.

Recruitment Terms

Screening
The process of checking whether a candidate meets the basic job requirements.
Technical Round
An interview or assessment that checks technical knowledge and problem-solving skills.
HR Round
A discussion about communication, personality, expectations and company policies.
Offer
An official job proposal given to a selected candidate.
