Welcome to Shimla

Shimla is a beautiful hill destination famous for its mountains, forests, colonial buildings & pleasant weather.

It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India. Visitors can enjoy nature, local food, shopping & adventure.

Popular Attractions in Shimla

  • The Ridge
  • Mall Road
  • Jakhoo Temple
  • Kufri
  • Christ Church
  • Summer Hill
  • Tara Devi Temple
  • Green Valley

Why Visit Shimla?

Shimla offers a combination of natural beauty, history, culture and adventure.

Visitors can enjoy mountain views, explore Mall Road and experience the peaceful atmosphere of the hills.

Travel Tip

Carry warm clothes during winter because temperatures can become very low.

Keep your travel bookings and important documents safe.

One-Day Shimla Itinerary

  1. Start your morning at The Ridge.
  2. Visit Christ Church.
  3. Walk around Mall Road.
  4. Enjoy local food.
  5. Visit Jakhoo Temple.
  6. End your day by enjoying the mountain view.
