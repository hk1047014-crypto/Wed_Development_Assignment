Important Career Terms

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals meet companies and explore career opportunities.
Internship
A structured learning opportunity that provides practical experience in a professional environment.
Placement
The process through which students are selected for jobs.
Workshop
An interactive learning session focused on a specific skill or topic.
Certification
A document showing successful completion of a course or assessment.
Networking
Building professional relationships with people and organizations.
