Personal Finance Education

Manage Your Money Wisely

Personal finance is about managing your income, expenses, savings & financial goals responsibly.

A good financial plan can help you understand where your money goes and how you can prepare for future needs.

"Do not save what is left after spending, but spend what is left after saving." — Warren Buffett

Understanding Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps people keep their money securely while earning interest.

Current Account

A current account is commonly used for frequent financial transactions by businesses and professionals.

Understanding Banking Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows users to make payments using money available in their bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card allows users to make purchases using an approved credit limit. Responsible repayment is important.

Understanding Loans

What Is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a financial institution that is generally repaid according to agreed terms.

Before taking a loan, understand the interest rate, repayment period & applicable charges.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save regularly for future goals.
  • Track your income & expenses.
  • Understand loan terms before borrowing.
  • Keep your banking information private.
  • Review your financial goals regularly.

How to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank and account type.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Provide the required documents.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Receive your account details.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on borrowed or deposited money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment — a fixed monthly payment generally used for loan repayment.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The agreed period for a loan, investment or financial product.
