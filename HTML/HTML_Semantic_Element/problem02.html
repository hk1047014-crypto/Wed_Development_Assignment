Our Hiring Process

  1. Submit your online application.
  2. Application screening by our recruitment team.
  3. Technical assessment or interview.
  4. HR and culture-fit discussion.
  5. Final selection and offer.

Recruitment Terms

Screening
The initial review of a candidate's application, qualifications and skills.
Technical Round
An interview or assessment focused on job-related technical skills.
HR Round
A discussion about communication, workplace expectations and cultural fit.
Offer
A formal proposal from the company to selected candidates.
