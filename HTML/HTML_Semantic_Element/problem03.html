How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select your preferred department.
  2. Choose a doctor and preferred date.
  3. Select a convenient consultation time.
  4. Provide your basic contact information.
  5. Submit your appointment request.
  6. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.

Important Patient Terms

Consultation
A scheduled discussion between a patient and a healthcare professional.
Follow-up
A later appointment arranged to review a patient's progress after an earlier consultation.
OPD
Outpatient Department, where patients receive consultation without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time for a patient to meet a doctor or specialist.

Patient Instructions

Please arrive a few minutes before your scheduled appointment.

Bring relevant previous medical reports or prescriptions if available.

Important: Appointment requests are subject to doctor availability and clinic confirmation.

For urgent or emergency situations, please contact the appropriate emergency services instead of relying on this appointment form.
