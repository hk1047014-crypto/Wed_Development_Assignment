Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a large event created for students, graduates and job seekers.

Participants can interact with universities, training institutes and employers to understand career paths, higher education, internships and placement opportunities.

Registration is now open for all eligible participants.

Event Objectives

  • Connect students with employers and universities.
  • Provide internship and placement information.
  • Explain scholarships and higher education options.
  • Provide career guidance and professional advice.
  • Help participants understand industry expectations.
"A career fair is not only about finding an opportunity; it is also about discovering possibilities and building meaningful connections."
— Event Organizing Team

Preparation Guide

Preparing in advance can help you get the maximum benefit from the career fair.

Preparation Tips

  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Wear appropriate professional clothing.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Keep academic and professional documents ready.
  • Attend relevant workshops and sessions.

Important Tips & Notices

Tip: Arrive early to avoid long registration queues.

Important Notice: Registration does not guarantee employment, admission, scholarship or internship selection.

Participants should verify opportunity details directly with participating organizations.

Your Career Fair Journey

  1. Register online for the event.
  2. Receive your registration reference.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete check-in.
  4. Explore participating organization booths.
  5. Attend workshops and information sessions.
  6. Speak with recruiters and university representatives.
  7. Submit resumes or contact details where appropriate.
  8. Follow up with relevant organizations after the event.

Information & Important Terms

Career Fair
An event where job seekers, students, employers and educational organizations interact.
Internship
A short-term work opportunity that provides practical professional experience.
Placement
The process through which candidates may be selected for employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on specified criteria.
Apprenticeship
A structured learning opportunity combining practical work experience with skill development.
