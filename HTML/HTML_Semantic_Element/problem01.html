Eligibility Criteria

Candidates should meet the academic requirements of their selected program. For most undergraduate programs, students must have passed class 12th from a recognized board.

Some science and engineering programs may require subjects such as Mathematics and Physics. Minimum eligibility criteria can vary from one program to another.

How to Apply

  1. Select your preferred program.
  2. Complete the online application form.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Review and submit your application.
  5. Pay the application fee if applicable.
  6. Wait for the admission or merit list result.

Important Admission Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic requirements required for admission to a program.
Merit List
A list of candidates selected based on academic performance or admission criteria.
Scholarship
Financial support provided to eligible students based on merit or other criteria.
Application Fee
The fee paid by a candidate while submitting an admission application.
